Face real pace, swing and spin. How many can you score?

SwipeSwipe the way you want to hit as the ball arrives. Up is straight, right is the off side, left is the leg side.
Loft itA long swipe hits it in the air for a six, but fielders can catch it. A short flick stays on the ground.
DefendTap to block. Don't swipe at wide balls, and protect your stumps.
TimingA ring closes in on the ball. Start your swipe as it closes.
