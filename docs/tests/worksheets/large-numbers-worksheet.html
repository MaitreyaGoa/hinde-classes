Before You Start

✦Quick Concept Notes

Prerequisite Recap

  • Place value: ones, tens, hundreds, thousands, ten-thousands.
  • A number's place value = digit × value of its position.
  • Commas are already used in 5-digit numbers, e.g. 45,830.
  • Every place is 10 times the place to its right.

The Big Idea

  • 999 (largest 3-digit) + 1 = 1,000 (smallest 4-digit)
  • 9,999 + 1 = 10,000
  • 99,999 + 1 = 1,00,000 = One Lakh
  • Pattern: each new "smallest number" is always 1 followed by zeroes.

Comma rule: Indian system → group in 3, then 2, 2, 2… from the right (thousand, lakh, crore). American system → group in 3, 3, 3… from the right (thousand, million, billion). Example: 9,87,65,01,234 (Indian) = 9,876,501,234 (American).

Rounding & Estimation

  • Round up — approx. is more than actual (e.g. ordering sweets: 732 → 750).
  • Round down — approx. is less than actual (e.g. pricing: ₹470 → ₹450).
  • Nearest neighbour — round to nearest 1000 / 10,000 / lakh / 10 lakh / crore.

Multiplication Shortcuts

  • ×5 = ÷2 then ×10
  • ×25 = ÷4 then ×100
  • ×125 = ÷8 then ×1000
  • n-digit × m-digit gives an (n+m−1)-digit or (n+m)-digit number.
