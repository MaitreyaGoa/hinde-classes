Section A · Basic Proportionality Theorem⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • If DE ∥ BC in △ABC (D on AB, E on AC), then ADDB = AEEC.
  • Converse: if ADDB = AEEC, then DE ∥ BC.
WORKED EXAMPLE
DE ∥ BC, AD = 3 cm, DB = 4 cm, AE = 6 cm. Find EC.
→ 34 = 6EC.
→ EC = 8 cm.
  1. A1AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm, AE = 5 cm. Find EC.
  2. A2ADDB = 35 and AC = 5.6 cm. Find AE.
  3. A3AD = x, DB = x − 2, AE = x + 2, EC = x − 1 and DE ∥ BC. Find x.
  4. A4AD = 1.5 cm, DB = 3 cm, AE = 1 cm, EC = 2 cm. Is DE ∥ BC?
Section B · Similarity Criteria⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • AA: two pairs of equal angles. SSS: all sides in the same ratio. SAS: one equal angle and the sides around it in the same ratio.
  • Corresponding sides of similar triangles are in the same ratio.
WORKED EXAMPLE
△ABC ~ △PQR, AB = 6 cm, PQ = 9 cm, BC = 8 cm. Find QR.
→ Ratio = 96 = 32.
→ QR = 8 × 32 = 12 cm.
  1. B1Triangles with angles 50°, 60°, 70° and 60°, 70°, 50°. Similar?

  3. B3Sides 3, 4, 6 and 9, 12, 15. Similar?
  4. B4△ABC ~ △DEF with AB = 4 cm, DE = 6 cm and EF = 9 cm. Find BC.
Section C · Shadows and Heights⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Sun's rays make equal angles at the same time → the triangles are similar.
  • Height ÷ shadow is the same for both objects.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A 6 m pole casts a 4 m shadow. A tower casts a 28 m shadow at the same time. Find its height.
→ h28 = 64.
→ h = 42 m.
  1. C1A 1.5 m stick casts a 1 m shadow. A building casts a 12 m shadow. Find the building's height.
  2. C2A girl 90 cm tall is 4.8 m from the foot of a 3.6 m lamp post. Find the length of her shadow.
  3. C3In △ABC, DE ∥ BC with AD = 2 cm, AB = 6 cm and BC = 9 cm. Find DE.
  4. C4A 12 m tree casts a 9 m shadow. How long is the shadow of a 2 m man at the same time?
Section D · Problems on Similar Triangles⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Name the similar triangles in the correct order, then write the ratio of sides.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Diagonals of trapezium ABCD (AB ∥ DC) meet at O. AO = 4, OC = 6, BO = 6. Find OD.
→ △AOB ~ △COD (AA), so AOOC = BOOD.
→ 46 = 6OD → OD = 9.

  2. D2△ABC ~ △PQR with perimeters 36 cm and 24 cm. If PQ = 10 cm, find AB.
  3. D3In △ABC, P and Q are on AB and AC with AP = 3, PB = 6, AQ = 5, QC = 10. Is PQ ∥ BC? Find PQ : BC.
