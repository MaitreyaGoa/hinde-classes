Section A · Solve by Factorisation⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Bring to ax² + bx + c = 0, split the middle term (product ac, sum b), factorise, set each factor = 0.
  • Clear fractions first by multiplying by the LCM (note the values x cannot take).
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve x − 6x = 1, x ≠ 0.
→ Multiply by x: x² − 6 = x → x² − x − 6 = 0.
→ (x − 3)(x + 2) = 0, so x = 3 or −2.
  1. A1x² + 5x − 36 = 0
  2. A23x² − 10x + 8 = 0
  3. A3x + 1x = 265
  4. A42x² + 9x + 10 = 0
  5. A51x − 2 + 2x − 1 = 6x, x ≠ 0, 1, 2
Section B · Formula and Discriminant⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • x = −b ± √D2a where D = b² − 4ac.
  • D > 0: two distinct real roots; D = 0: equal roots; D < 0: no real roots.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the nature of the roots of 4x² − 4x + 1 = 0.
→ D = 16 − 16 = 0.
→ Two equal real roots (x = 12).
  1. B1Solve 2x² − 4x − 3 = 0.
  2. B2Solve x² + 4x − 1 = 0.
  3. B3Nature of roots: 3x² + 2x + 5 = 0
  4. B4Find k so that x² − 2kx + 16 = 0 has equal roots.
  5. B5Find k so that kx² + 6x + 1 = 0 has equal roots.
Section C · Word Problems⏱ 45 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Let the unknown be x, form the equation, solve, and reject answers that do not make sense.
WORKED EXAMPLE
The sum of a number and its square is 156. Find the number.
→ x² + x − 156 = 0 → (x + 13)(x − 12) = 0.
→ x = 12 or −13.
  1. C1The product of two consecutive even positive integers is 168. Find them.
  2. C2A rectangle's length is 5 m more than its breadth and its area is 84 m². Find its dimensions.
  3. C3The sum of the squares of two consecutive natural numbers is 365. Find them.
  4. C4A train travels 360 km at a uniform speed. If the speed were 5 km/h more, it would take 1 hour less. Find the speed.
  5. C5The difference of the squares of two numbers is 45 and the square of the smaller is 4 times the larger. Find the numbers.
  6. C6A man's age 3 years ago times his age 4 years from now is 60. Find his present age.
Section D · Revision Mix⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Choose the quickest method: factorisation if it splits easily, else the formula.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve x² = 2x.
→ x² − 2x = 0 → x(x − 2) = 0.
→ x = 0 or 2. (Never divide by x!)
  1. D1x² − 7x = 0
  2. D2(x − 3)² = 25
  3. D3Is x = −12 a root of 2x² + 3x + 1 = 0?
