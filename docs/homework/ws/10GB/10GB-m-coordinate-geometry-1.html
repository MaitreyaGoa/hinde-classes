Section A · Distance Formula⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Distance between (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂) = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²).
  • Three points are collinear if the sum of the two smaller distances equals the largest.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the distance between (2, 3) and (10, −3).
→ √(8² + 6²) = √100.
→ = 10.
  1. A1(−5, 7) and (−1, 3)
  2. A2(a, b) and (−a, −b)
  3. A3Are (1, 5), (2, 3), (−2, −11) collinear?
  4. A4Find the point on the x-axis equidistant from (2, −5) and (−2, 9).
  5. A5Find y if the distance between (2, −3) and (10, y) is 10.
  6. A6Show that (1, 7), (4, 2), (−1, −1), (−4, 4) are the vertices of a square.
Section B · Section Formula⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Point dividing (x₁, y₁)–(x₂, y₂) in ratio m : n = (mx₂ + nx₁m + n, my₂ + ny₁m + n).
  • Mid-point = (x₁ + x₂2, y₁ + y₂2). For an unknown ratio, take k : 1.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the point dividing (−1, 7) and (4, −3) in the ratio 2 : 3.
→ x = 8 − 35 = 1, y = −6 + 215 = 3.
→ (1, 3).
  1. B1Mid-point of (−4, 6) and (8, −2)
  2. B2Point dividing (4, −3) and (8, 5) in the ratio 3 : 1
  3. B3In what ratio does (−4, 6) divide the join of (−6, 10) and (3, −8)?
  4. B4In what ratio does the x-axis divide the join of (1, −5) and (−4, 5)? Find the point.
  5. B5Find the points of trisection of the join of (2, −2) and (−7, 4).
Section C · Applications⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other: their mid-points are the same point.
WORKED EXAMPLE
The centre of a circle is (2, −3) and one end of a diameter is (1, 4). Find the other end.
→ Centre is the mid-point: (1 + x2, 4 + y2) = (2, −3).
→ x = 3, y = −10: (3, −10).
  1. C1(1, 2), (4, y), (x, 6), (3, 5) are the vertices of a parallelogram in order. Find x and y.
  2. C2Find the length of the median through A of △ABC with A(4, −6), B(3, −2), C(5, 2).
  3. C3Find the point on the y-axis equidistant from (5, −2) and (−3, 2).
  4. C4What type of triangle has vertices (0, 0), (3, 0), (0, 4)? Find its sides.
