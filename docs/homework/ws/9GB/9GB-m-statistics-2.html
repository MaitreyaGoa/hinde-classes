Section A · Median⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Arrange the data in ascending order first.
  • n odd: median = value in position n + 12.
  • n even: median = average of the values in positions n2 and n2 + 1.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the median of 7, 3, 9, 5, 11.
→ Order: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 (n = 5).
→ 3rd value = 7.
  1. A112, 7, 15, 9, 10, 14, 8
  2. A225, 30, 18, 22, 40, 35
  3. A345, 52, 38, 60, 52, 47, 55, 50
  4. A4The data 29, 32, 48, 50, x, x + 2, 72, 78, 84, 95 is in ascending order and its median is 63. Find x.
Section B · Mode⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Mode = the value that occurs most often. Data can have more than one mode.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the mode of 2, 3, 5, 3, 7, 3, 8.
→ 3 occurs three times – the most.
→ Mode = 3.
  1. B114, 25, 14, 28, 18, 17, 18, 14, 23, 22, 14, 18
  2. B2Shoe sizes: 6, 7, 8, 7, 9, 7, 6, 8, 7
  3. B3Marks 10, 20, 30, 40 with frequencies 3, 7, 5, 2. Find the mode.
  4. B4Can data have two modes? Give an example.
Section C · Mean of a Frequency Table⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Mean = ΣfxΣf: multiply each value by its frequency, add, divide by the total frequency.
  • If an unknown frequency p is involved, form an equation in p.
WORKED EXAMPLE
x: 1, 2, 3 with f: 2, 3, 5. Find the mean.
→ Σfx = 2 + 6 + 15 = 23, Σf = 10.
→ Mean = 2.3.
  1. C1x: 10, 20, 30, 40 with f: 4, 6, 8, 2. Find the mean.
  2. C2x: 5, 10, 15, 20 with f: 4, p, 6, 2. The mean is 12. Find p.
  3. C3The mean of 5 numbers is 15. A sixth number is added and the mean becomes 16. Find it.
  4. C4The mean of 25 observations was 36. Later it was found that 32 was wrongly copied as 23. Find the correct mean.
Section D · Mean, Median and Mode Together⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Find all three and compare. One very large or small value changes the mean a lot but not the median.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the mean, median and mode of 3, 5, 5, 7, 10.
→ Mean = 30 ÷ 5 = 6.
→ Median = 5, mode = 5.
  1. D12, 4, 4, 5, 6, 9
  2. D210, 12, 12, 15, 16, 19, 21
  3. D3Runs in 8 innings: 25, 0, 48, 25, 60, 12, 25, 85. Find mean, median and mode.
  4. D4In the previous question, which average best describes a typical innings? Why?
Section E · Word Problems⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Total = mean × number of values.
WORKED EXAMPLE
The mean of 6, 8, x, 12, 14 is 10. Find x.
→ Total = 50; 6 + 8 + 12 + 14 = 40.
→ x = 10.
  1. E1The mean weight of 6 students is 45 kg. One leaves and the mean of the rest is 44 kg. Find the weight of the one who left.
  2. E2Find the median of the first 8 prime numbers.
  3. E3Find the mean of the first 10 natural numbers.
