Section A · Cones – Mixed⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • l² = r² + h²; CSA = πrl; TSA = πr(l + r); V = 13πr²h. Use π = 227 unless told.
  • Find the missing measure first (r, h or l), then the asked quantity.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A cone has diameter 14 cm and slant height 25 cm. Find its height and volume.
→ r = 7, h = √(625 − 49) = 24 cm.
→ V = 13 × 227 × 49 × 24 = 1232 cm³.
  1. A1A cone has radius 6 cm and height 8 cm. Find its TSA. (π = 3.14)
  2. A2The volume of a cone is 9856 cm³ and its base diameter is 28 cm. Find its height.
  3. A3The CSA of a cone is 4070 cm² and its diameter is 70 cm. Find its slant height.
  4. A4A conical tent is 10 m high with base radius 24 m. Find its slant height and the cost of canvas at ₹70 per m².
  5. A5A heap of wheat is a cone of diameter 10.5 m and height 3 m. Find its volume.
Section B · Spheres and Hemispheres – Mixed⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Sphere: SA = 4πr², V = 43πr³. Hemisphere: CSA = 2πr², TSA = 3πr², V = 23πr³.
  • 1000 cm³ = 1 L.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the volume of a sphere of diameter 42 cm.
→ r = 21 cm.
→ V = 43 × 227 × 21³ = 38808 cm³.
  1. B1Find the surface area of a sphere of radius 10.5 cm.
  2. B2Find the volume of a hemispherical bowl of radius 3.5 cm.
  3. B3A hemispherical bowl of inner radius 5 cm is to be tin-plated inside at ₹16 per 100 cm². Find the cost. (π = 3.14)
  4. B4The radius of a balloon increases from 7 cm to 14 cm. Find the ratio of its surface areas.
  5. B5How many litres of milk can a hemispherical bowl of diameter 10.5 cm hold?
Section C · Comparing and Changing Sizes⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Write both formulas, then divide one by the other – the π cancels.
  • Doubling a length multiplies area by 4 and volume by 8.
WORKED EXAMPLE
The radius of a sphere is tripled. How many times does its volume become?
→ V ∝ r³, so 3³ = 27 times.
  1. C1Find the ratio of the volumes of two spheres whose radii are 2 : 3.
  2. C2The diameter of the Moon is about 14 of the Earth's. What fraction of the Earth's surface area is the Moon's?
  3. C3A cone and a hemisphere have equal bases and equal volumes. Find the ratio of their heights.
  4. C4A cone has radius r and height 2r. A sphere has radius r. Find the ratio of their volumes.
Section D · Applications⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Draw a rough figure and label r, h, l.
  • Decide: covering/painting → surface area; holding/filling → volume.
WORKED EXAMPLE
How much canvas is needed for a conical tent of radius 7 m and slant height 15 m?
→ CSA = 227 × 7 × 15 = 330 m².
  1. D1A dome is a hemisphere of inner diameter 21 m. Find the cost of painting it inside at ₹12 per m².
  2. D2A solid sphere of radius 21 cm is made of metal weighing 9 g per cm³. Find its mass in kg.
  3. D3A conical pit of top diameter 3.5 m is 12 m deep. What is its capacity in kilolitres?
  4. D4Twelve solid spheres each of radius 2 cm are melted. What total volume of metal is obtained?
  5. D5A hemispherical tank of radius 1.75 m is full of water. It is emptied by a pipe at 7 L per second. How long does it take to empty?
