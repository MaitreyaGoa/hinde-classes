Section A · Perimeter of Rectangles and Squares⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Perimeter = total length of the boundary.
  • Rectangle: P = 2 × (length + breadth). Square: P = 4 × side.
  • Both sides must be in the same unit. Write the unit in the answer.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the perimeter of a rectangle 12 cm long and 7 cm wide.
→ P = 2 × (12 + 7) = 2 × 19.
→ P = 38 cm.
  1. A1Find the perimeter of a rectangle 15 m by 9 m.
  2. A2Find the perimeter of a square of side 13 cm.
  3. A3The perimeter of a square is 64 cm. Find its side.
  4. A4A rectangle has perimeter 50 cm and length 15 cm. Find its breadth.
  5. A5Meena runs 3 rounds of a park 80 m long and 55 m wide. How far does she run?
Section B · Perimeter of Triangles and Polygons⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Add the lengths of all the sides.
  • A regular polygon has all sides equal: P = number of sides × side.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A triangle has sides 5 cm, 7 cm and 9 cm. Find its perimeter.
→ P = 5 + 7 + 9 = 21 cm.
  1. B1Find the perimeter of a triangle with sides 6 cm, 8 cm and 10 cm.
  2. B2Find the perimeter of an equilateral triangle of side 9 cm.
  3. B3Find the perimeter of a regular hexagon of side 5 cm.
  4. B4The perimeter of an equilateral triangle is 39 cm. Find its side.
  5. B5An isosceles triangle has two equal sides of 8 cm and perimeter 26 cm. Find the third side.
Section C · Area of Rectangles and Squares⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Area = space inside a shape, measured in square units (cm², m²).
  • Rectangle: A = length × breadth. Square: A = side × side.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the area of a rectangle 9 cm by 6 cm.
→ A = 9 × 6 = 54 cm².
  1. C1Find the area of a room 14 m by 8 m.
  2. C2Find the area of a square of side 11 cm.
  3. C3A rectangle has area 96 cm² and length 12 cm. Find its breadth.
  4. C4A square has area 81 cm². Find its side and its perimeter.
  5. C5How many square tiles of side 50 cm are needed for a floor 6 m by 4 m?
Section D · Area of Triangles⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • A diagonal cuts a rectangle into two equal right triangles, so a right triangle has half the rectangle's area.
  • Area of a triangle = 12 × base × height (height is perpendicular to the base).
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the area of a right triangle whose two shorter sides are 6 cm and 8 cm.
→ A = 12 × 6 × 8 = 24 cm².
  1. D1Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 7 cm.
  2. D2Find the area of a right triangle whose shorter sides are 12 cm and 5 cm.
  3. D3A rectangle 10 cm by 6 cm is cut along a diagonal. Find the area of each triangle.
  4. D4Find the area of a triangle with base 9 m and height 4 m.
Section E · Word Problems⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Boundary, fence, border, running round → perimeter.
  • Covering, painting, tiles, carpet → area.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the cost of fencing a garden 20 m by 15 m at ₹25 per metre.
→ P = 2 × (20 + 15) = 70 m.
→ Cost = 70 × 25 = ₹1,750.
  1. E1Find the cost of carpeting a room 5 m by 4 m at ₹150 per m².
  2. E2A wire is bent into a square of side 12 cm. It is then bent into a rectangle 15 cm long. Find the breadth. Which shape has more area?
  3. E3Two rectangles have perimeter 24 cm each: one is 8 cm by 4 cm, the other 6 cm by 6 cm. Find both areas. What do you notice?
  4. E4A square of side 10 cm is cut into 4 equal squares. Find the perimeter of one small square and the total of all four perimeters.
  5. E5A square field has perimeter 120 m. Find its area.
