Section A · Equivalent Fractions and Simplest Form⏱ 15 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Divide numerator and denominator by their HCF to get the simplest form.
  • To find a missing number, see what the known denominator (or numerator) was multiplied by.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Simplify 3648.
→ HCF of 36 and 48 is 12.
→ 3648 = 34.
  1. A1Simplify 4864.
  2. A2Simplify 84126.
  3. A3Find x: 37 = x56
  4. A4Are 1421, 1015 and 2233 equivalent? Show why.
Section B · Comparing Fractions⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Use a common denominator (LCM) to compare.
  • To see which is closer to 1, compare how much each is less than 1.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Which is closer to 1: 45 or 56?
→ 1 − 45 = 15, 1 − 56 = 16.
→ 16 < 15, so 56 is closer to 1.
  1. B1Arrange in ascending order: 710, 35, 1115, 23
  2. B2Which is closer to 1: 78 or 89?
  3. B3Which is bigger: 1316 or 45?
  4. B4How much less than 1 is 59?
Section C · Find the Missing Fraction⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • □ + a = b means □ = b − a.
  • □ − a = b means □ = b + a.
  • Check your answer by putting it back.
WORKED EXAMPLE
13 + □ = 34. Find □.
→ □ = 34 − 13 = 912 − 412 = 512.
  1. C114 + □ = 56
  2. C2□ − 23 = 16
  3. C312 + □ = 910
  4. C4213 − □ = 112

Section E · Word Problems⏱ 40 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Find the fraction first, then the quantity if asked.
  • 'The rest' means 1 minus the fractions already used.
WORKED EXAMPLE
14 of a class like music and 13 like dance. The rest like sports. What fraction like sports?
→ 14 + 13 = 712.
→ 1 − 712 = 512 like sports.
  1. E1Meena spends 12 of her pocket money on books and saves 15. What fraction is left? If she gets ₹200, how much is left?
