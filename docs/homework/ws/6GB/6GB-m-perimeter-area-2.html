Section A · Area of Combined Shapes⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Split the shape into rectangles, find each area, then add.
  • Or: area of the big rectangle minus the part cut out.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A rectangle 10 cm by 8 cm has a 4 cm by 3 cm piece cut from one corner. Find the area left.
→ 80 − 12 = 68 cm².
  1. A1A room 12 m by 10 m has a 4 m by 4 m corner cut off for a cupboard. Find the floor area left.
  2. A2A shape is made of a rectangle 8 cm by 3 cm placed on top of a rectangle 12 cm by 4 cm. Find its area.
  3. A3A photo frame is 10 cm by 8 cm outside and 8 cm by 6 cm inside. Find the area of the frame.
  4. A4A square of side 12 cm has a square hole of side 4 cm in the middle. Find the area left.
Section B · Perimeter of Combined Shapes⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Walk round the outside edge and add every side.
  • Cutting a rectangle from a corner does not change the perimeter (the two new edges replace the two removed ones).
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the perimeter of the 10 cm by 8 cm rectangle with a 4 cm by 3 cm corner removed.
→ Edges: 10, 5, 4, 3, 6, 8 → total 36 cm.
→ Same as the full rectangle: 2 × 18 = 36 cm.
  1. B1Find the perimeter of the 12 m by 10 m room with a 4 m by 4 m corner cut off.
  2. B2Two squares of side 5 cm are joined side by side. Find the perimeter of the new shape.
  3. B3Three squares of side 4 cm are placed in a row. Find the perimeter.
  4. B4Five unit squares form a plus (+) shape. Find its perimeter.
Section C · Paths and Borders⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Path area = area of outer rectangle − area of inner rectangle.
  • A path OUTSIDE adds twice its width to each side; a path INSIDE takes away twice its width.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A garden 20 m by 15 m has a 2 m wide path all round outside it. Find the area of the path.
→ Outer: 24 m by 19 m = 456 m².
→ Path = 456 − 300 = 156 m².
  1. C1A park 30 m by 20 m has a 1 m wide path inside along its edges. Find the area of the path.
  2. C2Find the cost of paving the path in Q1 at ₹20 per m².
  3. C3A photo 18 cm by 12 cm has a 1 cm border all round outside. Find the area of the border.
  4. C4Two paths, each 2 m wide, cross through the middle of a field 40 m by 30 m, parallel to the sides. Find the area of the paths.
Section D · What Happens When We Change the Sides?⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Work out the new measure and compare it with the old one.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A rectangle is 6 cm by 4 cm. What happens to its area if the length is doubled?
→ Old: 24 cm². New: 12 × 4 = 48 cm².
→ The area doubles.
  1. D1If both sides of the 6 cm by 4 cm rectangle are doubled, find the new area. How many times bigger is it?
  2. D2If the side of a square is tripled, how many times does its perimeter become? Its area?
  3. D3A square has side 5 cm. A rectangle has the same perimeter and length 7 cm. Which has more area?
Section E · Word Problems⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Draw a rough figure and write the measurements on it.
WORKED EXAMPLE
How many 5 cm by 5 cm squares can be cut from a sheet 30 cm by 20 cm?
→ 6 squares along the length, 4 along the breadth.
→ 6 × 4 = 24 squares.
  1. E1A wall 5 m by 3 m has a door 2 m by 1 m. Find the area to be painted.
  2. E2A field has area 2,400 m² and length 60 m. Find its breadth and perimeter.
  3. E3How many tiles of 20 cm by 20 cm cover a floor 4 m by 3 m?
  4. E4A rectangular plot is 25 m by 16 m. Find the cost of levelling it at ₹12 per m².
