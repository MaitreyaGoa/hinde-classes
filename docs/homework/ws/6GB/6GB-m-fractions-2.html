Section A · Fractions as Equal Shares⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • When some things are shared equally, each share = (number of things) ÷ (number of people), written as a fraction.
  • 3 rotis shared equally by 4 children: each gets 34 roti.
  • To compare two shares, compare the fractions (make the denominators the same).
WORKED EXAMPLE
5 laddoos are shared equally by 3 friends. How much does each get?
→ Each gets 5 ÷ 3 = 53 laddoos.
→ 53 = 123, so each gets 123 laddoos.
  1. A13 rotis are shared equally by 4 children. How much roti does each child get?
  2. A25 chikkis are shared equally by 2 children. How much does each get? Write as a mixed number.
  3. A37 apples are shared equally by 3 friends. How much does each get?
  4. A4Group A: 2 rotis shared by 3 children. Group B: 3 rotis shared by 5 children. In which group does each child get more?
  5. A54 children share 3 cakes equally. What fraction of a cake does each child get?
Section B · Fraction of a Collection⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • To find ab of a number: divide the number by b, then multiply by a.
  • Always write the unit (students, minutes, ₹) in the answer.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find 23 of 30 marbles.
→ 30 ÷ 3 = 10.
→ 10 × 2 = 20 marbles.
  1. B1Find 13 of 24.
  2. B2Find 34 of 20.
  3. B3Find 25 of 35.
  4. B4In a class of 40 students, 38 are girls. How many girls and how many boys are there?
  5. B5How many minutes make 56 of an hour?
Section C · Fractions on the Number Line and in Order⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • To mark ab on a number line, divide each unit into b equal parts and count a parts from 0.
  • An improper fraction lies between two whole numbers: change it to a mixed number to see which.
  • To order fractions, write them with a common denominator (LCM) and compare numerators.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Between which two whole numbers does 114 lie?
→ 114 = 234.
→ So it lies between 2 and 3.
  1. C1Between which two whole numbers does 175 lie?
  2. C2Draw a number line from 0 to 1 and mark 34 and 14 on it.
  3. C3Which fraction lies exactly halfway between 14 and 34?
  4. C4Arrange in descending order: 58, 34, 23, 712
  5. C5Write one fraction that is greater than 25 but less than 35.
Section D · Adding and Subtracting Mixed Numbers⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Change mixed numbers to improper fractions.
  • Use the LCM of the denominators to make like fractions, then add or subtract the numerators.
  • Change the answer back to a mixed number if it is improper.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find 112 + 223.
→ 32 + 83 = 96 + 166 = 256.
→ 256 = 416.
  1. D1234 + 156
  2. D25 − 223
  3. D3312 − 134
  4. D412 + 13 + 16
  5. D578 − 14 + 12
Section E · Word Problems⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Underline the numbers and what is asked.
  • Joining → add; taking away or 'how much more' → subtract.
  • Write the fraction sentence, solve, and give the answer with units.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Ravi drank 12 L of milk and 13 L of juice. How much did he drink in all?
→ 12 + 13 = 36 + 26 = 56.
→ He drank 56 L.
