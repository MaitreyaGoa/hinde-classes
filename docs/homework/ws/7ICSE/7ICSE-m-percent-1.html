HOW TO SOLVE

Section E · Increase, Decrease and Word Problems⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • New value = old × (100 + increase%) ÷ 100, or old × (100 − decrease%) ÷ 100.
  • Percent change = changeoriginal × 100.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A price of ₹800 increases by 15%. Find the new price.
→ 800 × 115100 = ₹920.
