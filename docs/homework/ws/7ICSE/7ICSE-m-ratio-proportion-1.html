Section A · Ratio in Simplest Form⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Both quantities must be in the same unit.
  • Divide both terms by their HCF. Remove decimals by multiplying both terms by 10 or 100.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Express 1.5 kg : 600 g in simplest form.
→ 1500 g : 600 g.
→ Divide by 300: 5 : 2.
  1. A148 : 72
  2. A22 m : 75 cm
  3. A345 minutes : 2 hours
  4. A4₹3.50 : ₹1.40
  5. A50.6 : 1.5
Section B · Dividing in a Given Ratio⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Add the terms of the ratio to get the total parts.
  • Each share = (its term ÷ total parts) × amount.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Divide ₹1,200 in the ratio 3 : 5.
→ Total parts 8; one part = ₹150.
→ Shares ₹450 and ₹750.

  3. B3The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find them.
  4. B4In a class of 48, boys and girls are in the ratio 7 : 5. How many of each?

Section D · Proportion⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • a : b :: c : d means a × d = b × c (product of extremes = product of means).
  • Fourth proportional to a, b, c: d = b × ca. Third proportional to a, b: b²a. Mean proportional between a and c: √(ac).
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the fourth proportional to 4, 6 and 10.
→ 4 : 6 :: 10 : x.
→ x = 6 × 104 = 15.
  1. D1Are 3, 5, 12, 20 in proportion?
  2. D2Find the fourth proportional to 2, 7 and 6.
  3. D3Find the third proportional to 4 and 6.
  4. D4Find the mean proportional between 4 and 9.
  5. D5Find x: 5 : x :: 15 : 27
Section E · Word Problems⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Let the quantities be ak and bk, form an equation, find k.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 8 and their sum is 104. Find them.
→ 5k + 8k = 104 → k = 8.
→ Numbers 40 and 64.
  1. E1The ages of A and B are in the ratio 4 : 5. B is 25 years old. Find A's age.
  2. E2Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 7. When 4 is added to each, the ratio becomes 1 : 2. Find the numbers.
  3. E3A map has scale 1 : 50,000. Two places are 6 cm apart on it. Find the real distance.
  4. E4A man's income and expenditure are in the ratio 9 : 7. He saves ₹4,000 a month. Find his income.
