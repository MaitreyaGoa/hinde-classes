Section A · Mixed Equations⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Open brackets, clear fractions with the LCM, collect like terms, divide.
  • Check every answer by substitution.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve 2(3x − 1) − x + 42 = 5.
→ Multiply by 2: 4(3x − 1) − (x + 4) = 10.
→ 12x − 4 − x − 4 = 10, 11x = 18, x = 1811.

Section B · Digit and Fraction Problems⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • A two-digit number with tens digit t and units digit u is 10t + u.
  • For a fraction problem, let numerator = x and write the denominator in terms of x.
WORKED EXAMPLE
The digits of a two-digit number add to 9. The tens digit is twice the units digit. Find the number.
→ Units u, tens 2u: u + 2u = 9, u = 3.
→ Number = 63.
  1. B1The tens digit of a two-digit number is 3 more than the units digit, and the digits add to 11. Find the number.
  2. B2The denominator of a fraction is 4 more than its numerator. If 1 is added to both, it becomes 12. Find the fraction.
  3. B3The numerator of a fraction is 6 less than the denominator. If 3 is added to the numerator it becomes 23. Find the fraction.
  4. B4A number is divided into two parts so that one part is 23 of the other. The number is 40. Find the parts.
Section C · Speed, Work and Everyday Problems⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Distance = speed × time.
  • Profit = selling price − cost price.
  • Read the question again after solving – does your answer make sense?
WORKED EXAMPLE
A car goes at 60 km/h. How long does it take for 210 km?
→ 210 = 60 × t.
→ t = 3.5 hours.
  1. C1A train covers a distance in 4 hours at 75 km/h. How long would it take at 60 km/h?
  2. C2A shopkeeper buys a toy and sells it for ₹540, making a profit of ₹90. Find the cost price.
  3. C3Riya has ₹50 more than Tina. Together they have ₹350. How much does each have?
  4. C4The perimeter of an equilateral triangle is 3 cm more than the perimeter of a square of side 8 cm. Find the side of the triangle.
  5. C5After spending 25 of her money and then ₹60 more, Asha has ₹120 left. How much did she have?
