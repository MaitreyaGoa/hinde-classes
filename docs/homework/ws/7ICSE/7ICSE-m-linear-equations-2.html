Section A · Transposition⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Moving a term to the other side changes its sign: + becomes −, × becomes ÷.
  • Collect letter terms on the left and numbers on the right, then divide.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve 9x − 4 = 5x + 16.
→ 9x − 5x = 16 + 4.
→ 4x = 20, so x = 5.
  1. A13x + 7 = x + 19
  2. A211 − 2y = 3y + 1
  3. A36(x − 1) = 4(x + 2)
  4. A45 − 3(2 − p) = 2p + 9
  5. A50.5x + 3 = 0.2x + 6
  6. A61.2y − 0.4 = 0.8y + 1.2
Section B · Equations with Fractions⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Multiply EVERY term by the LCM of the denominators.
  • Put brackets around numerators with two terms before multiplying.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve x + 12 − x − 13 = 2.
→ LCM 6: 3(x + 1) − 2(x − 1) = 12.
→ 3x + 3 − 2x + 2 = 12, so x = 7.
  1. B12x3 − 4 = x2
  2. B23x − 24 = x + 42
  3. B3x4 + x6 = x − 7
  4. B4y + 35 + y − 13 = 4
  5. B52m + 13 − m − 44 = 3
Section C · Number and Age Problems⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Let the unknown be x and write the others in terms of x.
  • For ages: 'after n years' add n; 'n years ago' subtract n.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A number is 12 more than another. Their sum is 50. Find them.
→ x + (x + 12) = 50.
→ 2x = 38, x = 19. Numbers 19 and 31.
  1. C1One number is 3 times another. Their difference is 26. Find them.
  2. C2When 7 is subtracted from 4 times a number, the result is 3 more than twice the number. Find the number.
  3. C3The sum of three consecutive odd numbers is 63. Find them.
  4. C4A father is 30 years older than his son. In 5 years he will be 3 times as old as the son. Find their present ages.
  5. C5Five years ago, a woman's age was 4 times her daughter's. Their present ages add up to 50. Find the present ages.
Section D · Money and Measurement⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Money: (number of notes) × (value of each note) = amount.
  • Perimeter of rectangle = 2(length + breadth); angles of a triangle add to 180°.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A pen costs ₹8 more than a pencil. 2 pens and 3 pencils cost ₹46. Find the cost of each.
→ Pencil x: 2(x + 8) + 3x = 46.
→ 5x = 30, x = 6. Pencil ₹6, pen ₹14.
  1. D1A bag has ₹5 and ₹2 coins, 30 coins in all, worth ₹90. How many of each?
  2. D2The length of a rectangle is twice its breadth and its perimeter is 72 cm. Find its length and breadth.
  3. D3Two angles of a triangle are equal and the third is 30° more than each of them. Find the angles.
  4. D4Two supplementary angles differ by 40°. Find them.
  5. D5₹1000 is divided between A and B so that A gets ₹200 more than B. Find each share.
