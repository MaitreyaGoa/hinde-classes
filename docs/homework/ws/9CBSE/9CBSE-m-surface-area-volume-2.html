Section A · Cones⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • l² = r² + h², CSA = πrl, TSA = πr(l + r), V = 13πr²h.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A cone has r = 7 cm, h = 24 cm. Find l, CSA, TSA and volume.
→ l = 25 cm; CSA = 227 × 7 × 25 = 550 cm².
→ TSA = 704 cm²; V = 13 × 227 × 49 × 24 = 1232 cm³.
  1. A1r = 21 cm, h = 28 cm: slant height, CSA, volume
  2. A2A cone has r = 14 cm and l = 50 cm. Find its height and CSA.
  3. A3Find the volume of a cone with r = 3.5 cm and h = 12 cm.
  4. A4A conical tent has radius 24 m and height 7 m. Find the canvas needed.
Section B · Spheres and Hemispheres⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Sphere: SA = 4πr², V = 43πr³. Hemisphere: CSA = 2πr², TSA = 3πr², V = 23πr³.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the SA and volume of a sphere of radius 7 cm.
→ SA = 4 × 227 × 49 = 616 cm².
→ V = 43 × 227 × 343 = 143713 cm³.
  1. B1Sphere of radius 14 cm: SA and volume
  2. B2TSA of a solid hemisphere of diameter 21 cm
  3. B3Capacity of a hemispherical bowl of radius 10.5 cm, in litres
  4. B4A sphere has surface area 154 cm². Find its radius.
Section C · Melting and Recasting⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • When a solid is melted and recast, the VOLUME stays the same. Equate volumes; π cancels.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A metal sphere of radius 6 cm is melted into a cylinder of radius 4 cm. Find its height.
→ 43π × 216 = π × 16 × h.
→ h = 18 cm.
  1. C1A cone of radius 6 cm and height 24 cm is melted into a sphere. Find its radius.
  2. C2How many balls of radius 1 cm can be made from a sphere of radius 4 cm?
  3. C3A cylinder of radius 3 cm and height 10 cm is melted into cones of radius 3 cm and height 2 cm. How many cones?
Section D · Combined Solids⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Split into simple solids. Volume: add. Surface area: add only the outside surfaces.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A toy is a cone (r = 3.5 cm, h = 12 cm) on a hemisphere of the same radius. Find its volume.
→ Cone = 154 cm³; hemisphere = 23 × 227 × 42.875 = 5396 cm³.
→ Total = 14636 ≈ 243.83 cm³.
  1. D1A capsule is a cylinder with a hemisphere at each end. Total length 14 mm, diameter 5 mm. Find its surface area.
  2. D2A vessel is a hemisphere of radius 7 cm with a cylinder of height 6 cm on top. Find its volume.
  3. D3A solid cylinder (r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm) has a cone of the same radius and height scooped out. Find the volume left.
