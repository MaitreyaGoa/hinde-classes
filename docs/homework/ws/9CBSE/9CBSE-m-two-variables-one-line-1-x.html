Section A · Linear Equations and Their Solutions⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • ax + by + c = 0 (a, b not both 0) is a linear equation in two variables. It has infinitely many solutions (x, y).
  • To find a solution, choose x, then work out y.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Write three solutions of 2x + y = 7.
→ x = 0 → y = 7; x = 1 → y = 5; x = 3 → y = 1.
→ (0, 7), (1, 5), (3, 1).
  1. A1Write 3x = 5y − 2 in the form ax + by + c = 0.
  2. A2Is (2, −1) a solution of 3x + 4y = 2?
  3. A3Write any three solutions of x − 2y = 4.
  4. A4Find k if (1, 3) lies on kx − y = 5.
  5. A5Pens cost ₹x and pencils ₹y. 3 pens and 2 pencils cost ₹50. Write the equation and two possible prices.
Section B · Slope-Intercept Form y = mx + d⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Rewrite ax + by + c = 0 as y = −abx − cb. Then m is the slope and d the y-intercept.
  • Slope through (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂): m = y₂ − y₁x₂ − x₁.
  • m = −5 means: when x increases by 1, y decreases by 5.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the slope and y-intercept of 5x + y = 3.
→ y = −5x + 3.
→ m = −5, d = 3: the line meets the y-axis at (0, 3).
  1. B1Find m and d for y = 4x − 7.
  2. B2Find m and d for 2x + 3y = 12.
  3. B3Find the slope of the line through (1, 2) and (4, 11).
  4. B4Find the equation y = mx + d of the line with slope 2 through (0, −3).
  5. B5Find the equation of the line through (2, 5) and (4, 9).
  6. B6Are y = 3x + 1 and y = 3x − 4 parallel? Why?
Section C · Graphs⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Make a table of at least three points, plot them and draw a straight line through them.
  • x-intercept: put y = 0. y-intercept: put x = 0.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Draw y = 2x − 1.
→ x = 0 → −1; x = 1 → 1; x = 2 → 3.
→ Plot (0, −1), (1, 1), (2, 3) and join.
  1. C1Draw x + y = 5. Where does it meet the axes?
  2. C2Draw y = −x + 2 and y = x on the same axes. Where do they meet?
  3. C3Draw 2x − y = 4. Find its slope from the graph.
  4. C4A taxi charges ₹30 plus ₹12 per km. Write y (fare) in terms of x (km) and find the fare for 8 km.
Section D · Real-Life Lines⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Identify what x and y stand for, write the equation, then answer the question.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A plant is 5 cm tall and grows 2 cm per week. Write its height h after w weeks.
→ h = 2w + 5.
→ After 6 weeks: 17 cm.
  1. D1Water is 40 L in a tank and drains at 4 L per minute. Write the amount y after x minutes. When is it empty?
  2. D2Celsius and Fahrenheit: F = 95C + 32. Find F when C = 35, and C when F = 50.
  3. D3A mobile plan costs ₹150 a month plus ₹2 per GB. Write the cost y for x GB. How many GB for a ₹200 bill?
Section E · Extra Practice⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Use slope and intercepts together.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the area of the triangle formed by 2x + 3y = 12 and the axes.
→ Intercepts (6, 0) and (0, 4).
→ Area = 12 × 6 × 4 = 12 sq units.
  1. E1Find the area of the triangle formed by x + 2y = 8 and the axes.
  2. E2For what k is the line y = (k − 1)x + 4 parallel to y = 3x?
  3. E3Find the point on y = 3x − 2 whose y-coordinate is twice its x-coordinate.
