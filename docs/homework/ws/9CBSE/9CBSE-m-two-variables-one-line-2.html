Section A · Substitution Method⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • From one equation, write one variable in terms of the other.
  • Substitute into the other equation, solve, then find the second variable.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve x + y = 7, x − y = 3.
→ x = 7 − y.
→ 7 − y − y = 3 → y = 2, x = 5.
  1. A1y = 2x, x + y = 9
  2. A2x − 2y = 1, 2x + y = 12
  3. A33x + y = 10, x + 2y = 5
  4. A42x − 3y = 1, x + y = 3
Section B · Elimination Method⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Multiply the equations so one variable has the same coefficient.
  • Add or subtract to eliminate it, solve, then substitute back.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve 2x + 3y = 13, 3x − y = 3.
→ Multiply the second by 3: 9x − 3y = 9.
→ Add: 11x = 22, x = 2; then y = 3.
  1. B1x + y = 10, x − y = 4
  2. B23x + 2y = 16, x − 2y = 0
  3. B34x + 3y = 24, 2x − y = 2
  4. B45x − 2y = 11, 3x + 4y = 4
Section C · How Many Solutions?⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Compare a₁a₂, b₁b₂, c₁c₂.
  • a₁a₂ ≠ b₁b₂: unique solution (lines intersect). a₁a₂ = b₁b₂ ≠ c₁c₂: no solution (parallel). All equal: infinitely many (coincident).
WORKED EXAMPLE
How many solutions does x − y = 10, 10x − 10y = 101 have?
→ 110 = −1−10 but 10 ≠ 101 ÷ 10, i.e. 10101 ≠ 110.
→ Parallel lines – no solution.
  1. C12x + y = 6 and x − y = 2
  2. C2x + y = 4 and 2x + 2y = 8
  3. C3x − 2y = 4 and 2x − 4y = 6
  4. C4For what k does kx + 2y = 5, 3x + y = 1 have no solution?
  5. C5For what k do x + 2y = 3 and 3x + ky = 9 have infinitely many solutions?
Section D · Graphical Solution⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Draw both lines on the same axes.
  • The meeting point is the solution; parallel lines → no solution.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve graphically x + y = 4, x − y = 2.
→ Line 1: (0, 4), (4, 0); line 2: (2, 0), (0, −2).
→ They meet at (3, 1).
  1. D1Solve graphically x + 3y = 6 and 2x − 3y = 12.
  2. D2Draw x + 2y = 4 and 2x + 4y = 12. What do you notice?
Section E · Word Problems⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Choose two variables, write two equations from the two facts, solve.
WORKED EXAMPLE
2 erasers and 3 sheets cost ₹9; 3 erasers and 2 sheets cost ₹11. Find each price.
→ 2x + 3y = 9, 3x + 2y = 11.
→ Add: x + y = 4; subtract: x − y = 2 → x = 3, y = 1.
  1. E1The sum of two numbers is 50 and their difference is 14. Find them.
  2. E25 apples and 3 bananas cost ₹95; 2 apples and 4 bananas cost ₹80. Find the cost of each.
  3. E3A rectangle's perimeter is 40 cm and its length is 4 cm more than its breadth. Find its sides.
  4. E4A fraction becomes 12 when 1 is added to both its numerator and denominator, and 13 when 1 is subtracted from both. Find it.
