Section A · Angles of Quadrilaterals⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Angles of a quadrilateral add to 360°.
  • In a parallelogram, opposite angles are equal and adjacent angles add to 180°.
WORKED EXAMPLE
The angles of a quadrilateral are x, 2x, 3x, 4x. Find them.
→ 10x = 360 → x = 36.
→ 36°, 72°, 108°, 144°.
  1. A1Three angles are 75°, 90°, 110°. Find the fourth.
  2. A2Angles in the ratio 3 : 5 : 9 : 13. Find them.
  3. A3One angle of a parallelogram is 70°. Find the others.
  4. A4Adjacent angles of a parallelogram are (2x + 10)° and (3x − 20)°. Find them.
Section B · Properties⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Parallelogram: opposite sides equal, diagonals bisect each other.
  • Rectangle: + equal diagonals. Rhombus: + diagonals perpendicular and bisect the angles. Square: all of these.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Is every square a rhombus?
→ A square has 4 equal sides, so yes.
  1. B1True or false: every rhombus is a square.
  2. B2True or false: every rectangle is a parallelogram.
  3. B3True or false: a parallelogram with equal diagonals is a rectangle.
  4. B4Name the quadrilaterals whose diagonals are perpendicular.
  5. B5Name the quadrilaterals whose diagonals are equal.
Section C · Using the Properties⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Write the property as an equation, then solve.
WORKED EXAMPLE
In parallelogram ABCD, diagonals meet at O. AO = (3x + 2) cm, OC = (5x − 6) cm. Find AC.
→ AO = OC → 3x + 2 = 5x − 6 → x = 4.
→ AO = 14, so AC = 28 cm.
  1. C1A rhombus has diagonals 16 cm and 30 cm. Find its side and perimeter.
  2. C2In rectangle ABCD, AC = (2x + 4) cm and BD = (3x − 1) cm. Find x and the diagonal.
  3. C3In rhombus ABCD, ∠ABC = 110°. Find ∠BAD and ∠ABD.
  4. C4The sides of a parallelogram are in the ratio 3 : 5 and its perimeter is 64 cm. Find the sides.
Section D · Mid-point Theorem⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • The segment joining the mid-points of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and half of it.
WORKED EXAMPLE
D, E are mid-points of AB, AC and BC = 12 cm. Find DE.
→ DE = 12 × 12 = 6 cm, and DE ∥ BC.
  1. D1A triangle has sides 8 cm, 10 cm, 12 cm. Find the perimeter of the triangle formed by joining the mid-points.
  2. D2D, E are mid-points of AB, AC and DE = 3.5 cm. Find BC.
  3. D3Why is the figure formed by joining the mid-points of the sides of any quadrilateral a parallelogram?
  4. D4What figure do the mid-points of the sides of a rectangle form?
Section E · Proofs⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • State the triangles, list equal parts with reasons, give the congruence rule, conclude.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Prove that the diagonals of a rectangle ABCD are equal.
→ In △ABC and △DCB: AB = DC, ∠ABC = ∠DCB = 90°, BC common.
→ SAS → △ABC ≅ △DCB, so AC = DB.
  1. E1Prove that if the diagonals of a parallelogram are equal, it is a rectangle.
  2. E2Prove that each diagonal of a parallelogram divides it into two congruent triangles.
