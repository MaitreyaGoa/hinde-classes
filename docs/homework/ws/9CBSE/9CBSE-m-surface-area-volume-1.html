Section A · Cuboid and Cube⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Cuboid: TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl), walls (LSA) = 2h(l + b), V = lbh.
  • Cube: TSA = 6a², V = a³. 1 m³ = 1000 L.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the TSA and volume of a cuboid 10 cm × 8 cm × 5 cm.
→ TSA = 2(80 + 40 + 50) = 340 cm².
→ V = 400 cm³.
  1. A1Cube of edge 7 cm: TSA and volume
  2. A2Cuboid 12 cm × 8 cm × 6 cm: TSA and volume
  3. A3A room is 5 m × 4 m × 3 m high. Find the area of its four walls.
  4. A4Find the cost of painting those walls at ₹30 per m².
  5. A5How many litres does a tank 2 m × 1.5 m × 1 m hold?
Section B · Cylinder⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • CSA = 2πrh, TSA = 2πr(r + h), V = πr²h. Use π = 227.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A cylinder has r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm. Find its CSA and volume.
→ CSA = 2 × 227 × 7 × 10 = 440 cm².
→ V = 227 × 49 × 10 = 1540 cm³.
  1. B1r = 14 cm, h = 20 cm: CSA, TSA, volume
  2. B2A pillar has diameter 28 cm and height 15 cm. Find its CSA.
  3. B3A cylinder of radius 7 cm has volume 1540 cm³. Find its height.
  4. B4A roller of diameter 84 cm and length 120 cm makes 500 turns to level a ground. Find the area levelled in m².
Section C · What Changes When Sizes Change?⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Write the formula with the new measures and compare.
WORKED EXAMPLE
The edge of a cube is doubled. How do its surface area and volume change?
→ SA: 6(2a)² = 4 × 6a² → 4 times.
→ V: (2a)³ = 8a³ → 8 times.
  1. C1The radius of a cylinder is doubled and its height halved. How does its volume change?
  2. C2How many cubes of edge 3 cm can be cut from a cube of edge 12 cm?
  3. C3How many bricks 25 cm × 12.5 cm × 7.5 cm are needed for a wall 5 m × 3 m × 25 cm?
  4. C4A cuboid 18 cm × 12 cm × 9 cm is cut into cubes of side 3 cm. How many cubes?
Section D · Word Problems⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Open box: no top face. Capacity in litres: m³ × 1000.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A cylindrical tank has radius 1.4 m and height 2 m. Find its capacity in litres.
→ V = 227 × 1.96 × 2 = 12.32 m³.
→ = 12,320 L.

  4. D4A cylindrical glass of radius 3.5 cm and height 10 cm is filled with juice. How many such glasses fill from 3.85 L?
