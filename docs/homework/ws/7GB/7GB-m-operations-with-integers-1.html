Section A · Multiplying Integers⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • (+) × (+) = +, (−) × (−) = +, (+) × (−) = (−) × (+) = −.
  • With many factors: an even number of negative signs gives +, an odd number gives −.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find (−3) × 4 × (−2) × (−1).
→ Three negative signs → the answer is negative.
→ 3 × 4 × 2 × 1 = 24, so the answer is −24.

Section B · Dividing Integers⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Same signs → positive quotient; different signs → negative quotient.
  • 0 ÷ (a non-zero integer) = 0. Division by 0 is not defined.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find (−56) ÷ 8.
→ Different signs → negative.
→ 56 ÷ 8 = 7, so the answer is −7.
  1. B1(−72) ÷ (−9)
  2. B263 ÷ (−7)
  3. B3(−100) ÷ 25
  4. B40 ÷ (−5)
  5. B5(−45) ÷ (−45)
  6. B6Find x if x ÷ (−4) = 6.
Section C · Smart Calculation with Properties⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Order and grouping do not change a product: a × b = b × a, (a × b) × c = a × (b × c).
  • Distributive: a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c. Use it to break numbers near 100.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find (−15) × 98.
→ (−15) × (100 − 2) = −1500 + 30.
→ = −1470.

  2. C253 × (−101)
  3. C3(−12) × 17 + (−12) × (−7)
  4. C4Is (−8) ÷ 4 equal to 4 ÷ (−8)? What does this show?
Section D · Missing Numbers⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Undo the operation: if □ × a = b then □ = b ÷ a.
  • Check the sign of your answer by multiplying back.
WORKED EXAMPLE
□ × (−6) = 48. Find □.
→ □ = 48 ÷ (−6) = −8.
  1. D1(−9) × □ = −81
  2. D2□ ÷ (−3) = −12
  3. D3The product of two integers is −36. One of them is 4. Find the other.
  4. D4□ × □ = 49, where □ is a negative integer.
Section E · Word Problems⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • A fall, loss, debt or going below is negative; a rise, profit or going up is positive.
WORKED EXAMPLE
The temperature is 12 °C and falls 3 °C every hour. What is it after 7 hours?
→ Change = 7 × (−3) = −21.
→ 12 + (−21) = −9 °C.
  1. E1In a test, +4 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong one. Ravi gets 12 correct and 8 wrong. Find his score.
  2. E2A submarine at −40 m goes down 15 m every minute for 6 minutes. Where is it now?
  3. E3A shopkeeper gains ₹6 on each pen and loses ₹3 on each pencil. He sells 40 pens and 50 pencils. Profit or loss?
