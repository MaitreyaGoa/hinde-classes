Section A · Congruent Figures and Corresponding Parts⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Figures with the same shape and size are congruent (≅): one fits exactly on the other, even after turning or flipping.
  • In △ABC ≅ △XYZ the order matters: A↔X, B↔Y, C↔Z, so AB = XY, BC = YZ, CA = ZX and ∠A = ∠X, ∠B = ∠Y, ∠C = ∠Z.
WORKED EXAMPLE
If △PQR ≅ △LMN, which side equals QR?
→ Q↔M and R↔N, so QR = MN.
  1. A1If △ABC ≅ △XYZ, write all three pairs of equal sides.
  2. A2If △ABC ≅ △XYZ, write all three pairs of equal angles.
  3. A3△DEF ≅ △PQR. Which angle equals ∠E? Which side equals DF?
  4. A4Is it correct to write △ACB ≅ △XYZ when △ABC ≅ △XYZ? Give the correct form.
  5. A5Are two squares of side 5 cm congruent? Are two rectangles with the same area always congruent?
Section B · Conditions for Congruent Triangles⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • SSS: all three sides equal. SAS: two sides and the angle BETWEEN them equal.
  • ASA: two angles and the side between them equal. AAS: two angles and a side not between them.
  • RHS: in right triangles, hypotenuse and one side equal.
  • Two sides and an angle NOT between them do not guarantee congruence.
WORKED EXAMPLE
AB = PQ = 5 cm, BC = QR = 6 cm, ∠B = ∠Q = 50°. Are △ABC and △PQR congruent?
→ ∠B is between AB and BC; ∠Q is between PQ and QR.
→ SAS holds, so △ABC ≅ △PQR.
  1. B1AB = 4 cm, BC = 5 cm, CA = 6 cm; XY = 4 cm, YZ = 5 cm, ZX = 6 cm. Which condition? Write the congruence.
  2. B2∠A = 40°, ∠B = 60°, AB = 7 cm; ∠P = 40°, ∠Q = 60°, PQ = 7 cm. Which condition?
  3. B3In right triangles ABC and DEF (right angles at B and E), AC = DF = 13 cm and BC = EF = 5 cm. Which condition?
  4. B4∠A = ∠X = 35°, ∠C = ∠Z = 75°, BC = YZ = 4 cm. Which condition? (Hint: find ∠B and ∠Y first.)
  5. B5AB = DE = 6 cm, AC = DF = 8 cm, ∠B = ∠E = 40°. Can we be sure the triangles are congruent? Why?
  6. B6Do three equal angles (AAA) guarantee congruence? Give a reason.
Section C · Using Congruence in Figures⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Look for a common side (it equals itself) and vertically opposite angles (they are equal).
  • Parallel lines give equal alternate angles.
  • Once triangles are congruent, the other corresponding parts are also equal.
WORKED EXAMPLE
ABCD is a rectangle. Are △ABD and △CDB congruent?
→ AB = CD, AD = CB (rectangle) and BD is common.
→ SSS holds: △ABD ≅ △CDB.
  1. C1O is the midpoint of AD and of BC. Show that △AOB ≅ △DOC. Which condition did you use?
  2. C2In the previous question, what can you say about AB and DC?
  3. C3PQRS is a square with diagonal PR. Show that △PQR ≅ △RSP.
  4. C4AB ∥ CD and AB = CD; AD and BC meet at O. Name the equal angles and the congruence.
  5. C5In △ABC, AB = AC and AD is the altitude to BC. Show that BD = DC.
Section D · Isosceles and Equilateral Triangles⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • In a triangle, angles opposite equal sides are equal.
  • Angles of a triangle add to 180°.
  • All angles of an equilateral triangle are 60°.
WORKED EXAMPLE
In △ABC, AB = AC and ∠A = 80°. Find ∠B and ∠C.
→ ∠B = ∠C (opposite equal sides).
→ ∠B + ∠C = 180° − 80° = 100°, so each is 50°.
  1. D1In △PQR, PQ = PR and ∠P = 40°. Find ∠Q and ∠R.
  2. D2An isosceles triangle has one base angle of 65°. Find the other two angles.
  3. D3The vertex angle of an isosceles triangle is 3 times each base angle. Find all three angles.
  4. D4Can an isosceles triangle have two angles of 100°? Why?
  5. D5What is each angle of an equilateral triangle? Why?
