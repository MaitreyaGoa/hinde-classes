Section A · Brackets and Order of Operations⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Brackets first, then × and ÷ (left to right), then + and −.
  • Watch every sign; write one step per line.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find (−3) × [6 − (−4)].
→ Bracket: 6 + 4 = 10.
→ (−3) × 10 = −30.
  1. A1(−5) + (−3) × 4
  2. A2(−48) ÷ (−6) + (−2) × 5
  3. A3[(−6) − (−10)] × (−3)
  4. A4(−36) ÷ [(−2) × (−3)]
  5. A515 − (−2) × (−7)
Section B · Sign Without Calculating⏱ 15 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Count the negative signs. Any factor 0 makes the product 0.
WORKED EXAMPLE
What is the sign of (−2) × (−5) × (−7) × 3?
→ Three negative factors → negative.
  1. B1The product of 7 negative integers is positive or negative?
  2. B2(−1) multiplied by itself 100 times
  3. B3(−2) × (−3) × 0 × (−5)
  4. B4A negative integer divided by a negative integer is…
Section C · Integer Puzzles⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • List factor pairs of the product, then check the sum.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find two integers whose product is −24 and sum is 2.
→ Pairs for −24: (6, −4) has sum 2.
→ Answer: 6 and −4.
  1. C1Product 18, sum −9
  2. C2Product −30, sum −1
  3. C3The product of three integers is −60. Two of them are −3 and −4. Find the third.
  4. C4Find an integer between −20 and 0 that is divisible by both 4 and 6.
Section D · Word Problems⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Write the number sentence with signs, then calculate.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A quiz gives +5 for correct, −2 for wrong, 0 for not attempted. Asha has 12 correct, 5 wrong, 3 not attempted. Score?
→ 12 × 5 + 5 × (−2) = 60 − 10 = 50.
  1. D1In the same quiz, Raju gets 4 correct and his score is −6. How many did he get wrong?
  2. D2At 5 pm the temperature is 8 °C. It falls 2 °C every hour. At what time will it be −4 °C?
  3. D3A company gains ₹8 on each bag of white cement and loses ₹5 on each bag of grey cement. It sells 3,000 white and 5,000 grey bags. Profit or loss?
  4. D4A mine cage starts 10 m above ground and goes down 6 m every minute. Where is it after 1 hour?
