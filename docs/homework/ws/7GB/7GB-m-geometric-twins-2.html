Section A · Which Condition?⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Mark equal parts on a rough figure.
  • Check SSS, SAS (angle between), ASA, AAS, RHS.
  • AAA and SSA (angle not between) do NOT prove congruence – write 'cannot say'.
WORKED EXAMPLE
AB = DE, ∠B = ∠E, BC = EF. Which condition?
→ ∠B is between AB and BC.
→ SAS: △ABC ≅ △DEF.
  1. A1PQ = XY = 5 cm, QR = YZ = 6 cm, ∠Q = ∠Y = 70°.
  2. A2∠A = ∠D = 50°, ∠B = ∠E = 60°, BC = EF = 8 cm.
  3. A3AB = DE = 7 cm, BC = EF = 9 cm, CA = FD = 11 cm.
  4. A4Right angles at C and F, AB = DE = 10 cm, AC = DF = 6 cm.
  5. A5∠A = ∠X, ∠B = ∠Y, ∠C = ∠Z.
  6. A6AB = PQ = 4 cm, AC = PR = 6 cm, ∠C = ∠R = 30°.
Section B · Finding Measures from a Congruence⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Match the letters in order: in △ABC ≅ △PQR, A↔P, B↔Q, C↔R.
  • Corresponding parts are equal – use this to find sides, angles or x.
WORKED EXAMPLE
△ABC ≅ △PQR, ∠A = 60°, ∠B = 70°. Find ∠R.
→ ∠R = ∠C = 180° − 60° − 70° = 50°.
  1. B1△ABC ≅ △PQR with AB = 5 cm, BC = 7 cm, CA = 6 cm. Find QR and PR.
  2. B2△DEF ≅ △XYZ with ∠D = 40°, ∠E = 75°. Find ∠Z.
  3. B3△LMN ≅ △RST with LM = (3x − 1) cm and RS = 11 cm. Find x.
  4. B4△ABC ≅ △DEF with ∠B = (2y + 10)° and ∠E = 50°. Find y.
  5. B5△ABC ≅ △PQR and AB = 4 cm, BC = 5 cm, CA = 6 cm. Find the perimeter of △PQR.
Section C · What More Is Needed?⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Write the condition you want, then name the missing equal part.
WORKED EXAMPLE
AB = PQ and BC = QR. What else is needed for SAS?
→ The angle between them: ∠B = ∠Q.
  1. C1AB = PQ and ∠A = ∠P. What is needed for SAS? For ASA?
  2. C2∠B = ∠E and ∠C = ∠F. What is needed for ASA?
  3. C3Right triangles ABC and PQR (right angles at B and Q) with AC = PR. What is needed for RHS?
  4. C4AB = DE and BC = EF. What is needed for SSS?
Section D · Congruence in Figures⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Name the two triangles, list three pairs of equal parts with reasons, state the condition.
  • Then use 'corresponding parts of congruent triangles' to conclude.
WORKED EXAMPLE
In quadrilateral ABCD, AB = AD and CB = CD. Show △ABC ≅ △ADC.
→ AB = AD, CB = CD (given), AC = AC (common).
→ SSS, so △ABC ≅ △ADC and ∠BAC = ∠DAC.
  1. D1Line l is the perpendicular bisector of PQ, meeting it at M. R is any point on l. Show RP = RQ.
  2. D2AB and CD bisect each other at O. Show AC = BD.
  3. D3In △ABC, AB = AC and D is the midpoint of BC. Show △ABD ≅ △ACD and AD ⊥ BC.
  4. D4PQRS is a rectangle with diagonals PR and QS. Show △PQR ≅ △QPS and hence PR = QS.
Section E · Isosceles Triangles⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Equal sides ↔ equal opposite angles.
  • Angle sum of a triangle = 180°.
WORKED EXAMPLE
In △ABC, AB = AC and ∠B = 55°. Find ∠A.
→ ∠C = 55°.
→ ∠A = 180° − 110° = 70°.
  1. E1In △PQR, PQ = PR, ∠Q = (x + 10)° and ∠R = (2x − 20)°. Find x and all the angles.
  2. E2Find the angles of an isosceles right triangle.
  3. E3Two angles of a triangle are 70° and 40°. Is it isosceles? Why?
