Section A · Slant Height of a Cone⏱ 10 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • l² = r² + h² (Pythagoras), where r = radius, h = height, l = slant height.
  • Learn: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 7-24-25, 8-15-17 triplets – they save time.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find l if r = 9 cm, h = 12 cm.
→ l² = 81 + 144 = 225, so l = 15 cm.
  1. A1r = 6 cm, h = 8 cm. Find l.
  2. A2r = 5 cm, h = 12 cm. Find l.
  3. A3l = 25 cm, r = 7 cm. Find h.
Section B · Surface Area of a Cone⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Curved Surface Area (CSA) = πrl. Total Surface Area (TSA) = πr(l + r).
  • Use π = 227 unless told otherwise. Cancel 7 first.
  • If h is given instead of l, first find l.
  • Units of area: cm², m².
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the CSA of a cone with r = 7 cm and l = 15 cm.
→ CSA = 227 × 7 × 15 = 22 × 15 = 330 cm².
  1. B1Find the CSA of a cone with r = 7 cm, l = 10 cm.
  2. B2Find the TSA of a cone with r = 14 cm, l = 20 cm.
  3. B3Find the CSA of a cone with r = 21 cm, h = 28 cm.
  4. B4A conical tent has radius 7 m and height 24 m. How much canvas is needed (CSA)?
  5. B5The CSA of a cone is 308 cm² and its slant height is 14 cm. Find its radius.
  6. B6The curved surface of a conical roof (r = 7 m, l = 12 m) is to be painted at ₹20 per m². Find the cost.
Section C · Volume of a Cone⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Volume = 13 πr²h. (h is the vertical height, NOT the slant height.)
  • 1000 cm³ = 1 litre.
  • Units of volume: cm³, m³.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the volume of a cone with r = 7 cm, h = 9 cm.
→ V = 13 × 227 × 49 × 9 = 22 × 7 × 3 = 462 cm³.
  1. C1r = 7 cm, h = 12 cm. Find the volume.
  2. C2r = 21 cm, h = 10 cm. Find the volume.
  3. C3A cone has h = 15 cm and l = 17 cm. Find its volume.
  4. C4A cone of volume 1232 cm³ has radius 7 cm. Find its height.
  5. C5A conical vessel has r = 14 cm, h = 30 cm. Find its capacity in litres.
Section D · Sphere⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Surface Area = 4πr². Volume = 43 πr³.
  • If diameter is given, radius = diameter ÷ 2.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the surface area of a sphere of radius 14 cm.
→ SA = 4 × 227 × 14 × 14 = 4 × 22 × 28 = 2464 cm².
  1. D1Find the surface area of a sphere of radius 7 cm.
  2. D2Find the surface area of a sphere of diameter 21 cm.
  3. D3Find the volume of a sphere of radius 21 cm.
  4. D4Find the volume of a sphere of radius 3.5 cm.
  5. D5The surface area of a sphere is 154 cm². Find its radius.
Section E · Hemisphere⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • CSA = 2πr² (only the curved bowl). TSA = 3πr² (bowl + flat circular top).
  • Volume = 23 πr³.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the TSA of a hemisphere of radius 14 cm.
→ TSA = 3 × 227 × 14 × 14 = 3 × 22 × 28 = 1848 cm².
  1. E1Find the CSA of a hemisphere of radius 7 cm.
  2. E2Find the TSA of a hemisphere of radius 7 cm.
  3. E3Find the volume of a hemisphere of radius 21 cm.
  4. E4A hemispherical bowl has radius 10.5 cm. How many litres of milk can it hold?
  5. E5Two spheres have radii in the ratio 2 : 3. Find the ratio of their surface areas.
Section F · Thinking Questions⏱ 15 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Write the formula in letters first, then compare or substitute.
  • For 'how many times', divide the new value by the old value.
WORKED EXAMPLE
The radius of a sphere is doubled. How many times does its surface area become?
→ Old = 4πr². New = 4π(2r)² = 16πr².
→ 16πr² ÷ 4πr² = 4 times.
  1. F1The radius of a sphere is doubled. How many times does its volume become?
  2. F2A sphere and a cone have the same radius 7 cm, and the cone's height is 14 cm. Find the ratio of their volumes.
  3. F3The inner curved surface of a hemispherical dome of diameter 14 m is painted at ₹40 per m². Find the cost.
