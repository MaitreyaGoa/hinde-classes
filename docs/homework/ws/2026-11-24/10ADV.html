Section A · Pair of Linear Equations – Higher Order⏱ 45 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Use substitution or elimination (the Board excludes cross-multiplication).
  • Equations in 1x, 1y or 1x+y: put u = 1x, v = 1y to make them linear.
  • Consistency: a₁a₂ ≠ b₁b₂ → unique; a₁a₂ = b₁b₂ ≠ c₁c₂ → no solution; all three equal → infinitely many.
  • In word problems, define both variables clearly and check that the answers make sense.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve 2x + 3y = 2 and 4x − 9y = −1.
→ Let u = 1x, v = 1y: 2u + 3v = 2 and 4u − 9v = −1.
→ Multiply the first by 3: 6u + 9v = 6. Add to the second: 10u = 5, so u = 12.
→ Then 3v = 2 − 1, so v = 13. Hence x = 2, y = 3.
  1. A1Solve 2x + 3y = 13 and 5x − 4y = −2.
  2. A2For what value of k does the pair kx + 3y = k − 3, 12x + ky = k have NO solution?
  3. A3Find a and b for which 2x + 3y = 7 and (a − b)x + (a + b)y = 3a + b − 2 have infinitely many solutions.
  4. A4A fraction becomes 1 if 1 is added to the numerator and 1 is subtracted from the denominator. It becomes 12 if 1 is only added to the denominator. Find the fraction.
  5. A5A boat goes 30 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 10 hours. In 13 hours it goes 40 km upstream and 55 km downstream. Find the speed of the stream and of the boat in still water.
  6. A6Ten years from now, A will be twice as old as B. Five years ago, A was three times as old as B. Find their present ages.
  7. A7Solve graphically 2x + y = 6 and 2x − y + 2 = 0. Find the area of the triangle formed by these lines and the x-axis.
Section B · Quadratic Equations – Higher Order⏱ 55 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Clear fractions by taking the LCM; state restrictions on x (denominator ≠ 0).
  • With surds or letters, use splitting the middle term (product = ac) or the formula.
  • For 'equal roots' / 'real roots' questions, work with D = b² − 4ac.
  • Time–speed–work problems: write time = distancespeed, work per hour = 1time.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve 1x − 1x − 2 = 3, x ≠ 0, 2.
→ LCM: (x − 2) − x = 3x(x − 2) → −2 = 3x² − 6x → 3x² − 6x + 2 = 0.
→ x = 6 ± √126 = 3 ± √33.
  1. B1Solve 1x + 4 − 1x − 7 = 1130, x ≠ −4, 7.
  2. B2Solve √2x² + 7x + 5√2 = 0.
  3. B3Solve abx² + (b² − ac)x − bc = 0.
  4. B4Find p for which (p + 1)x² − 6(p + 1)x + 3(p + 9) = 0, p ≠ −1, has equal roots.
  5. B5If −5 is a root of 2x² + px − 15 = 0 and px² + px + k = 0 has equal roots, find k.
  6. B6A train travels 480 km at a uniform speed. If the speed were 8 km/h less, it would take 3 hours more. Find the speed.
  7. B7Two taps together fill a tank in 938 hours. The larger tap takes 10 hours less than the smaller to fill it alone. Find the time each takes alone.
  8. B8Prove that the roots of (x − a)(x − b) = k² are real for all real a, b, k.
  9. B9A motorboat's speed in still water is 18 km/h. It takes 1 hour more to go 24 km upstream than to return downstream. Find the speed of the stream.
  10. B10Is it possible to design a rectangular park of perimeter 80 m and area 400 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.
Section C · Board Challenge⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • These need algebraic proof – write every step and give the final statement clearly.
WORKED EXAMPLE
If x = 1 is a common root of ax² + ax + 3 = 0 and x² + x + b = 0, find ab.
→ a + a + 3 = 0 → a = −32; 1 + 1 + b = 0 → b = −2.
→ ab = 3.
