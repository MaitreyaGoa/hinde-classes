Section A · Equivalent Fractions and Simplest Form⏱ 15 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • To get an equivalent fraction, multiply (or divide) the numerator AND the denominator by the SAME number.
  • To write a fraction in its simplest form, divide both numbers by their HCF (highest common factor).
  • Two fractions are equivalent if their simplest forms are the same.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Write 1218 in simplest form.
→ Factors common to 12 and 18: 1, 2, 3, 6. HCF = 6.
→ 12 ÷ 6 = 2 and 18 ÷ 6 = 3.
→ So 1218 = 23.

Section B · Comparing Fractions⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Same denominator: the fraction with the bigger numerator is bigger.
  • Same numerator: the fraction with the SMALLER denominator is bigger (fewer, bigger pieces).
  • Different numerators and denominators: change both to the same denominator using the LCM, then compare.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Which is bigger: 23 or 45?
→ LCM of 3 and 5 = 15.
→ 23 = 1015 and 45 = 1215.
→ 12 > 10, so 45 is bigger.

Section C · Mixed Numbers and Improper Fractions⏱ 15 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Improper → mixed: divide numerator by denominator. Quotient = whole part, remainder = new numerator, denominator stays the same.
  • Mixed → improper: (whole × denominator) + numerator, over the same denominator.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Convert 174 to a mixed number, and 235 to an improper fraction.
→ 17 ÷ 4 = 4 remainder 1, so 174 = 414.
→ 235 = 2 × 5 + 35 = 135.

  2. C2Convert 236 to a mixed number.
  3. C3Convert 423 to an improper fraction.
  4. C4Convert 278 to an improper fraction.
Section D · Adding and Subtracting Like Fractions⏱ 15 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Like fractions have the same denominator.
  • Add or subtract only the numerators. Keep the denominator the same.
  • Simplify the answer and change improper answers to mixed numbers.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find 59 + 79.
→ Numerators: 5 + 7 = 12, so the answer is 129.
→ Simplify: 129 = 43 = 113.

Section E · Adding and Subtracting Unlike Fractions⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Find the LCM of the denominators.
  • Change each fraction to an equivalent fraction with the LCM as denominator.
  • Now add or subtract the numerators. Simplify.
  • For mixed numbers, first change them to improper fractions.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find 34 + 16.
→ LCM of 4 and 6 = 12.
→ 34 = 912 and 16 = 212.
→ 912 + 212 = 1112.

Section F · Word Problems⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Read the question twice. Underline the numbers and the question being asked.
  • Decide: are we joining (add) or taking away / finding the difference (subtract)?
  • Write the fraction sentence, solve, and write the answer with its unit.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Meera drank 14 litre of milk in the morning and 23 litre at night. How much did she drink in all?
→ Joining → add: 14 + 23.
→ LCM = 12: 312 + 812 = 1112.
→ She drank 1112 litre.
