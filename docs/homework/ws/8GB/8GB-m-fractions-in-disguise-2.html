Section A · Successive Percentage Changes⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Each change multiplies by its own factor: +r% → × 100 + r100, −r% → × 100 − r100.
  • Multiply the factors; do NOT just add the percentages.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A price goes up 10% and then down 10%. What is the overall change?
→ 1.1 × 0.9 = 0.99.
→ Overall 1% decrease.
  1. A1Up 20%, then up 20% again. Overall change?
  2. A2Up 25%, then down 20%. Overall change?
  3. A3Up 5%, then up 10%. Overall change?
  4. A4Down 10%, then down 10%. Overall change?
Section B · Working Backwards⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • New = original × factor, so original = new ÷ factor.
WORKED EXAMPLE
After a 15% discount, a shirt costs ₹1,530. Find the marked price.
→ MP × 0.85 = 1530.
→ MP = ₹1,800.
  1. B1After a 12% increase, a price is ₹2,240. Find the original price.
  2. B2A phone is sold at a 25% loss for ₹900. Find the cost price.
  3. B3A village grew by 10% to 3,300 people. What was it before?
  4. B460% of a number is 45. Find the number.
Section C · Simple Interest⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • SI = P × R × T100; Amount = P + SI.
  • To find R or T, rearrange the formula.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the SI on ₹5,000 at 8% per year for 3 years.
→ SI = 5000 × 8 × 3100 = ₹1,200.
→ Amount = ₹6,200.
  1. C1SI on ₹12,000 at 7.5% per year for 2 years
  2. C2Amount on ₹8,000 at 6% per year for 5 years
  3. C3₹4,000 earns ₹960 SI in 3 years. Find the rate.
  4. C4In how many years will ₹2,500 earn ₹600 at 6% per year?
Section D · Tax and Mixtures⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Price with tax = price × 100 + tax%100.
  • Amount of a substance = its percentage × total.
WORKED EXAMPLE
An item costs ₹800 plus 18% GST. Find the bill.
→ 800 × 1.18 = ₹944.
  1. D1A bill of ₹1,500 has 12% GST added. Find the total.
  2. D2A price including 5% tax is ₹1,050. Find the price before tax.
  3. D320 L of salt water is 15% salt. How much salt is in it?
  4. D4How much water must be added to the 20 L so that it becomes 10% salt?
Section E · Challenge⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Compare using percentages of the same base.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A's salary is 25% more than B's. By what percent is B's salary less than A's?
→ Let B = 100, A = 125.
→ 25125 × 100 = 20%.
  1. E1Shop A: 'Buy 2 get 1 free'. Shop B: '30% off'. Which gives the bigger discount?
  2. E2The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percent must a family cut its use to spend the same?
  3. E3Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. What percentage of their total is each?
  4. E4A number is increased by 20% and the result is decreased by 20%. Is the final number more or less than the original, and by what percent?
