Section A · Ratios and Equivalent Ratios⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • A ratio a : b compares two quantities of the same kind (same units).
  • Simplest form: divide both terms by their HCF.
  • Equivalent ratios: multiply or divide both terms by the same number.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Write 45 cm : 2 m in simplest form.
→ 2 m = 200 cm, so 45 : 200.
→ HCF 5 → 9 : 40.
  1. A1Write 36 : 60 in simplest form.
  2. A2Write 250 g : 2 kg in simplest form.
  3. A3Write 40 minutes : 2 hours in simplest form.
  4. A4Fill in: 4 : 7 = 20 : □
  5. A5Are 6 : 9 and 10 : 15 equivalent?
Section B · Proportion and the Rule of Three⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • a : b = c : d is a proportion if a × d = b × c.
  • Rule of three: if a things cost b, then c things cost (b ÷ a) × c.
WORKED EXAMPLE
If 5 pens cost ₹60, what do 8 pens cost?
→ 1 pen costs 60 ÷ 5 = ₹12.
→ 8 pens cost 8 × 12 = ₹96.
  1. B1Are 3, 8, 15, 40 in proportion?
  2. B2Find x: 4 : x = 10 : 35
  3. B3If 12 notebooks cost ₹420, find the cost of 7 notebooks.
  4. B4A car uses 6 L of petrol for 84 km. How far will it go on 15 L?
  5. B5If 3 kg of rice costs ₹156, how much rice can you buy for ₹364?
Section C · Sharing in a Ratio⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • To divide N in the ratio a : b, total parts = a + b.
  • First share = aa + b × N, second share = ba + b × N.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Divide ₹600 in the ratio 2 : 3.
→ Total parts 5; one part = ₹120.
→ Shares ₹240 and ₹360.
  1. C1Divide ₹840 between Ravi and Sita in the ratio 3 : 4.
  2. C2Divide 72 sweets among three children in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4.
  3. C3The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 5 : 4. There are 360 students. How many girls are there?
  4. C4A 35 cm line is divided in the ratio 2 : 5. Find both parts.
  5. C5Two partners invest ₹40,000 and ₹60,000 and share a profit of ₹15,000 in the ratio of their investments. Find each share.
Section D · Unit Conversion and Rates⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Speed = distance ÷ time; convert km/h to m/s by × 518.
  • Use the rate for 1 unit, then multiply.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Convert 54 km/h into m/s.
→ 54 × 518 = 15.
→ 15 m/s.
  1. D1Convert 72 km/h into m/s.
  2. D2Convert 25 m/s into km/h.
  3. D3A tap fills 18 L in 3 minutes. How long to fill a 150 L tank?
  4. D4A map uses 1 cm : 5 km. Two towns are 7.5 cm apart on the map. How far apart are they really?
  5. D5Which is the better buy: 500 g of tea for ₹230 or 750 g for ₹330?
Section E · Extra Practice⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Find the value of one part first.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5. Find A : B : C.
→ Make B equal: 8 : 12 and 12 : 15.
→ A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15.
  1. E1A : B = 3 : 4 and B : C = 6 : 7. Find A : B : C.
  2. E2The ratio of two numbers is 3 : 5 and their difference is 18. Find the numbers.
