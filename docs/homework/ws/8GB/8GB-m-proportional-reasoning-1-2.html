Section A · Scaling Recipes and Mixtures⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Keep the ratio the same: multiply every part by the same number.
  • Part of a mixture = its ratio termtotal of terms × total.
WORKED EXAMPLE
3 cups of flour make 12 cookies. How much flour for 30 cookies?
→ Scale factor = 30 ÷ 12 = 2.5.
→ Flour = 3 × 2.5 = 7.5 cups.
  1. A1A recipe for 4 people uses 300 g of rice. How much rice for 10 people?
  2. A2Lemonade has juice : water = 1 : 6. How much water for 150 mL of juice?
  3. A3How much juice is in 1.4 L of this lemonade?
  4. A4Concrete is cement : sand : gravel = 1 : 2 : 4. How much sand is in 2.8 tonnes of concrete?
Section B · Maps and Scale Drawings⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Scale 1 : n means 1 cm on paper is n cm in real life.
  • Convert cm to m (÷ 100) or km (÷ 1,00,000).
WORKED EXAMPLE
On a map of scale 1 : 25,000, two towns are 8 cm apart. Find the real distance.
→ 8 × 25,000 = 2,00,000 cm.
→ = 2 km.
  1. B1Scale 1 : 2,00,000; map distance 4.5 cm. Real distance?
  2. B2Scale 1 : 5,00,000; real distance 15 km. Map distance?
  3. B3A model car is made at scale 1 : 40 and is 11 cm long. How long is the real car?
  4. B4On a plan, 1 cm stands for 2 m. A room is 4.5 cm by 3 cm on the plan. Find its real area.
Section C · Comparing Rates⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Find the rate for 1 unit (per km, per item, per minute), then compare.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A goes 12 km in 15 min; B goes 20 km in 30 min. Who is faster?
→ A: 48 km/h; B: 40 km/h.
→ A is faster.

Section D · Ratios That Change⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Write the quantities as ak and bk, apply the change, then equate to the new ratio.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. Adding 6 to each makes the ratio 3 : 4. Find them.
→ (3k + 6) : (5k + 6) = 3 : 4 → 12k + 24 = 15k + 18.
→ k = 2: numbers 6 and 10.
  1. D1Boys : girls = 4 : 5 in a group of 36. If 4 more boys join, what is the new ratio?
  2. D2The ages of two sisters are in the ratio 5 : 3. After 6 years the ratio will be 7 : 5. Find their ages.
  3. D340 L of a mixture has milk : water = 3 : 1. How much water must be added to make it 3 : 2?
  4. D4Three friends share a profit of ₹45,000 in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. Find each share.
Section E · Is It Proportional?⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Two quantities are in direct proportion if their ratio stays the same (y ÷ x is constant).
  • Check every pair in the table.
WORKED EXAMPLE
x: 2, 5, 8 and y: 6, 15, 24. Proportional?
→ 6 ÷ 2 = 15 ÷ 5 = 24 ÷ 8 = 3.
→ Yes, y = 3x.
  1. E1x: 3, 6, 9 and y: 12, 24, 36. Proportional? Write the rule.
  2. E2x: 2, 4, 6 and y: 5, 9, 13. Proportional?
  3. E3A table is proportional: x = 4, 10, □ and y = 14, □, 49. Fill in the blanks.
  4. E4Is a person's age proportional to their height? Explain.
