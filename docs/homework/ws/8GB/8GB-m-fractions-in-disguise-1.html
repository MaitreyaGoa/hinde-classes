Section A · Fractions and Percentages⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • A percentage is a fraction with denominator 100: z% = z100.
  • Fraction → percent: multiply by 100.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Express 34 as a percentage.
→ 34 × 100 = 75%.

Section B · Percentage of a Quantity⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • x% of y = x100 × y. Notice: x% of y = y% of x.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find 15% of 60 and 60% of 15.
→ Both = 15 × 60100 = 9.
  1. B1Find 40% of 160.
  2. B2Find 12.5% of 640.
  3. B3Find 8% of 25 and 25% of 8.
  4. B4In a school of 1,200 students, 45% are girls and 20% of the girls play hockey. How many girls play hockey?
Section C · Percentage Change⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Percentage change = changeoriginal value × 100.
  • Increase by r%: new = old × 100 + r100. Decrease: × 100 − r100.
WORKED EXAMPLE
A price rises from ₹40 to ₹46. Find the percentage increase.
→ Change = 6; 640 × 100 = 15%.
  1. C1250 increases to 300. Find the percentage increase.
  2. C2800 decreases to 680. Find the percentage decrease.
  3. C3Increase ₹1,200 by 8%.
  4. C4Decrease ₹2,500 by 12%.
  5. C5After a 20% increase, a price is ₹960. What was it before?
Section D · Profit, Loss and Discount⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Profit% = profitCP × 100; loss% = lossCP × 100.
  • Discount is on the marked price: SP = MP − discount.
WORKED EXAMPLE
CP ₹400, SP ₹460. Find the profit percent.
→ Profit = ₹60.
→ 60400 × 100 = 15%.
  1. D1CP ₹1,250, SP ₹1,100. Find the loss percent.
  2. D2Marked price ₹2,000, discount 15%. Find the selling price.
  3. D3The selling price of 4 pens equals the cost price of 5 pens. Find the profit percent.
  4. D4'Buy 1 get 1 free' – what is the percentage discount?
  5. D5'Buy 3 get 1 free' – what is the percentage discount?
Section E · Word Problems⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Read carefully: percent OF WHAT? That is the original (the 100%).
WORKED EXAMPLE
Asha scored 342 out of 450. Find her percentage.
→ 342450 × 100 = 76%.
  1. E1A salary of ₹25,000 rises by 10%, then by another 10%. Find the new salary and the total percentage increase.
  2. E2A rectangle's length increases by 25% and its area stays the same. By what percentage does the breadth decrease?
  3. E3A 50 g packet of nuts has 18% protein. How many grams of protein is that?
  4. E4A town had 40,000 people. 35% are children and 60% of the rest are adults under 60. How many adults are under 60?
