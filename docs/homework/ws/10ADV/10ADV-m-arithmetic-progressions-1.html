Section A · AP – Higher Order⏱ 55 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • aₙ = a + (n − 1)d, Sₙ = n2[2a + (n − 1)d], aₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁.
  • Three terms in AP: take a − d, a, a + d. Four terms: a − 3d, a − d, a + d, a + 3d.
WORKED EXAMPLE
If Sₙ = 3n² + 5n, find aₙ.
→ aₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁ = 3n² + 5n − 3(n − 1)² − 5(n − 1).
→ = 6n + 2.
  1. A1The sum of the first n terms of an AP is 4n − n². Find a₁, a₁₀ and the nth term.
  2. A2The sum of three numbers in AP is 27 and their product is 405. Find them.
  3. A3The 17th term of an AP exceeds its 10th term by 7. Find d.
  4. A4If the pth, qth and rth terms of an AP are a, b, c, show that a(q − r) + b(r − p) + c(p − q) = 0.
  5. A5Find the sum of all three-digit numbers that leave remainder 3 when divided by 5.
  6. A6If the sum of the first 7 terms of an AP is 49 and of the first 17 terms is 289, find the sum of the first n terms.
  7. A7The ratio of the sums of the first n terms of two APs is (7n + 1) : (4n + 27). Find the ratio of their 11th terms.
Section B · Problem Solving⏱ 45 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Turn the story into a, d and n first.
  • If n comes out as a fraction or negative, recheck or reject.
WORKED EXAMPLE
How many terms of 24, 21, 18, … give a sum of 78?
→ n2[48 − 3(n − 1)] = 78 → n² − 17n + 52 = 0.
→ n = 4 or 13 (both work: terms after the 4th add to 0).
  1. B1How many terms of the AP 9, 17, 25, … must be taken to give a sum of 636?
  2. B2A contract: penalty ₹200 for day 1, ₹250 for day 2, ₹300 for day 3, and so on. How much for 30 days of delay?
  3. B3In a school, prizes for 7 subjects total ₹700, each ₹20 less than the previous. Find the largest prize.
  4. B4Find the middle term of the AP 213, 205, 197, …, 37.
  5. B5A sum of ₹1,000 is invested at 8% simple interest per year. Do the interests at the end of each year form an AP? Find the interest after 30 years.
Section C · Board Challenge⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Write a clean proof with every step justified.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Show that the sum of an AP whose first term is a, second b and last c is (a + c)(b + c − 2a)(2(b − a)).
→ d = b − a, n = c − ab − a + 1.
→ S = n2(a + c); substitute n.
  1. C1If Sₙ denotes the sum of n terms of an AP, prove that S₁₂ = 3(S₈ − S₄).
  2. C2The angles of a triangle are in AP and the largest is twice the smallest. Find the angles.
