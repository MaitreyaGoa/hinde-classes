Section A · BPT and Similarity – Numericals⏱ 40 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Write the proportion from BPT or the similarity, cross-multiply, and check that lengths are positive.
WORKED EXAMPLE
DE ∥ BC, AD = 8x − 7, DB = 5x − 3, AE = 4x − 3, EC = 3x − 1. Find x.
→ (8x − 7)(3x − 1) = (5x − 3)(4x − 3) → 2x² − x − 1 = 0.
→ x = 1 (x = −12 gives negative lengths).
  1. A1DE ∥ BC with AD : DB = 3 : 5 and AC = 5.6 cm. Find AE.
  2. A2△ABC ~ △DEF with AB = 4 cm, DE = 6 cm, EF = 9 cm, FD = 12 cm. Find the perimeter of △ABC.
  3. A3D on BC of △ABC with ∠ADC = ∠BAC, BC = 12 cm, CD = 3 cm. Find CA.
  4. A4A 1.8 m man walks away from a 5.4 m lamp post at 1 m/s. Find his shadow after 6 s.
  5. A5AD = x + 1, DB = 3x − 1, AE = x + 3, EC = 3x + 4 and DE ∥ BC. Find x.
Section B · Proofs⏱ 45 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Name the triangles in matching order. Give a reason for every equal angle (vertically opposite, alternate, common).
WORKED EXAMPLE
Diagonals of trapezium ABCD (AB ∥ DC) meet at O. Prove AOBO = CODO.
→ ∠OAB = ∠OCD and ∠OBA = ∠ODC (alternate angles).
→ △AOB ~ △COD (AA) → AOCO = BODO → AOBO = CODO.
  1. B1S and T are on PQ and PR with PSSQ = PTTR and ∠PST = ∠PRQ. Prove △PQR is isosceles.
  2. B2ABC and AMP are right triangles with right angles at B and M. Prove △ABC ~ △AMP and CAPA = BCMP.
  3. B3In △ABC, AD ⊥ BC and AD² = BD × DC. Prove ∠BAC = 90°.
  4. B4E is on side AD produced of parallelogram ABCD and BE meets CD at F. Prove △ABE ~ △CFB.
Section C · Board Challenge⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Combine similarity with algebra.
WORKED EXAMPLE
△ABC ~ △PQR with perimeters 36 cm and 24 cm, PQ = 10 cm. Find AB.
→ Ratio of perimeters = ratio of sides = 3624 = 32.
→ AB = 15 cm.
  1. C1In △ABC, P and Q lie on AB and AC with AP = 3, PB = 6, AQ = 5, QC = 10. Show PQ ∥ BC and find PQ if BC = 12 cm.
  2. C2Two poles of 6 m and 11 m stand 12 m apart. Find the distance between their tops.
  3. C3Sides AB, BC and median AD of △ABC are proportional to sides PQ, QR and median PM of △PQR. Prove △ABC ~ △PQR.
  4. C4If a line divides two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, prove it is parallel to the third side (converse of BPT – outline).
