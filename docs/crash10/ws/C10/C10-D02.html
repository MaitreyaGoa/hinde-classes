Section A · 1-Mark Questions (VSA)⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • For ax² + bx + c with zeros α, β: α + β = −ba, αβ = ca.
  • A quadratic with zeros α, β: x² − (α + β)x + αβ.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the zeros of x² − 3x + 2.
→ (x − 1)(x − 2) = 0.
→ Zeros 1 and 2.
  1. A1Form a quadratic polynomial whose zeros are 3 and −5.
  2. A2Find the sum and product of the zeros of 2x² − 8x + 6.
  3. A3If 2 is a zero of x² + kx − 6, find k.
  4. A4If α, β are the zeros of x² − 5x + 6, find 1α + 1β.
  5. A5The graph of y = p(x) cuts the x-axis at 3 points. How many zeros does p(x) have?
Section B · Find the Zeros and Verify (2 marks)⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Split the middle term, set each factor = 0, then check sum and product with −ba and ca.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the zeros of x² − 2x − 8 and verify.
→ (x − 4)(x + 2) → zeros 4, −2.
→ Sum 2 = −−21 ✓, product −8 = −81 ✓.

Section C · Form the Polynomial (2 marks)⏱ 20 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Use k[x² − (sum)x + product] and choose k to clear fractions.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Sum of zeros −14, product 14. Find the polynomial.
→ x² + 14x + 14.
→ × 4: 4x² + x + 1.
  1. C1Sum √2, product 13
  2. C2Zeros 2 + √3 and 2 − √3
  3. C3Sum 0, product −5
Section D · Higher Order (3 marks)⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Write α + β and αβ first, then rewrite the expression using them: α² + β² = (α + β)² − 2αβ.
WORKED EXAMPLE
α, β are zeros of x² − 6x + 8. Find α² + β².
→ α + β = 6, αβ = 8.
→ 36 − 16 = 20.
  1. D1α, β are zeros of x² − 7x + 10. Find α² + β² and αβ + βα.
  2. D2For x² − (k + 6)x + 2(2k − 1), the sum of the zeros is half their product. Find k.
  3. D3One zero of 4x² − 8kx − 9 is the negative of the other. Find k.
  4. D4If one zero of 3x² + 11x + k is the reciprocal of the other, find k.
