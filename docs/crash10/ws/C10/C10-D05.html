Section A · Numbers, Ages and Fractions (3 marks)⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Choose two variables, write one equation for each fact, solve.
WORKED EXAMPLE
The sum of two numbers is 27 and their difference is 3. Find them.
→ x + y = 27, x − y = 3.
→ x = 15, y = 12.
  1. A1Five years ago, A was thrice as old as B. Ten years later, A will be twice as old as B. Find their present ages.
  2. A2A fraction becomes 911 if 2 is added to both its numerator and denominator, and 56 if 3 is added to both. Find it.
  3. A3The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. Nine times the number equals twice the number with digits reversed. Find it.
  4. A4Meena drew ₹2,000 in ₹50 and ₹100 notes, 25 notes in all. How many of each?
Section B · Money and Measurement (3 marks)⏱ 25 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Cost = number × rate. Perimeter and angle facts give the second equation.
WORKED EXAMPLE
5 pencils and 7 pens cost ₹50; 7 pencils and 5 pens cost ₹46. Find the cost of one of each.
→ 5x + 7y = 50, 7x + 5y = 46.
→ Add: x + y = 8; subtract: y − x = 2 → x = 3, y = 5.
  1. B1A taxi charges a fixed fare plus a rate per km: ₹105 for 10 km and ₹155 for 15 km. Find the fixed fare and rate. What is the fare for 25 km?
  2. B2The length of a rectangle is 3 cm more than its breadth; its perimeter is 46 cm. Find the sides.
  3. B3In △ABC, ∠C = 3∠B = 2(∠A + ∠B). Find the angles.
  4. B4A library charges a fixed amount for 3 days and extra per day after that. Saritha paid ₹27 for 7 days and Susy ₹21 for 5 days. Find the fixed charge and the daily charge.
Section C · Speed, Time and Distance (4 marks)⏱ 35 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Time = distancespeed. Downstream speed = u + v, upstream = u − v.
  • If the variables are in the denominator, put p = 1x, q = 1y.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve 2x + 3y = 13, 5x − 4y = −2.
→ p = 1x, q = 1y: 2p + 3q = 13, 5p − 4q = −2 → p = 2, q = 3.
→ x = 12, y = 13.
  1. C1A boat goes 30 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 10 hours, and 40 km upstream and 55 km downstream in 13 hours. Find the speed of the boat in still water and of the stream.
  2. C2Ritu rows 20 km downstream in 2 hours and 4 km upstream in 2 hours. Find her rowing speed and the speed of the current.
  3. C3Two places A and B are 100 km apart. Two cars start together from A and B. Going in the same direction they meet in 5 hours; going towards each other they meet in 1 hour. Find their speeds.
  4. C4A train covers a distance at a uniform speed. If it were 10 km/h faster it would take 2 hours less; if 10 km/h slower, 3 hours more. Find the distance.
Section D · Exam Practice⏱ 30 min
HOW TO SOLVE
  • Show every step: variables, equations, solving, answer with units.
WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve 12x + 13y = 2, 13x + 12y = 136.
→ p = 1x, q = 1y: p2 + q3 = 2, p3 + q2 = 136 → p = 2, q = 3.
→ x = 12, y = 13.
  1. D1The ratio of incomes of two people is 9 : 7 and of their expenditures 4 : 3. Each saves ₹2,000 a month. Find their monthly incomes.
  2. D2A two-digit number plus the number with its digits reversed is 66. The digits differ by 2. Find the number(s).
  3. D3Half the perimeter of a rectangular garden, whose length is 4 m more than its width, is 36 m. Find its dimensions.
